## Subset method
Enumerate all 2^11 = 2048 enabled-mechanism subsets if technically practical. The full v9.3 set is the reference. Every subset uses the same environment/event rules, deterministic policies, fixed capacities, and capability probes. An ablation removes the causal contribution of a mechanism without replacement behavior, privileged information, environment memory, renderer changes, or capacity changes. No subset is repaired or redesigned.

For each subset preserve: enabled mechanisms, structural validity, capability-family outcome vector, canonical state cost under normalized audit fixtures, and exact failed capabilities. Do not assume monotonicity. Multi-removal subsets are required even if all single ablations fail.

## Structural invalidity
A subset is structurally invalid only when the current architecture cannot be instantiated or executed with the requested mechanism disabled without violating the frozen ablation contract. Invalidity is not a behavioral failure and must be reported separately. Do not add adapters or replacement state to rescue an invalid subset.

## Full behavioral preservation
A subset has full historical capability coverage only when every required capability family in the frozen manifest passes. Raw test count and aggregate lifelikeness scores are not substitutes. Renderer invariance and canonical persistence/reconstruction remain mandatory architecture-level invariants.

## Capacity treatment
Capacity is held fixed at concern=3, prospective=2, eligibility=2 whenever the corresponding mechanism is enabled. Disabled mechanisms have no causal use of their representation. Capacity is not counted as a mechanism and is not tuned during this audit.

## Sealed holdouts
Before exhaustive results are inspected, create longitudinal interaction holdouts covering already-earned interactions only: relationship+affect; relationship+reliability; concern+prospective; concern competition+base pressures; subjective fact+learned action state without importing rejected EXP-008 semantics; habit+eligibility; and serialize/restore with multiple simultaneous persistent processes. Holdout expected outcomes are committed before subset results. Candidate minimal and near-minimal subsets are selected from the historical manifest first; holdouts are then opened without subset-specific optimization.

## Minimality decision
Find every smallest-cardinality subset with complete manifest coverage. If none below 11 exists, the local claim may be that all 11 are jointly required within this exact architecture, fixed capacities, and tested envelope. If smaller full-coverage subsets exist, independently replay all of them and test against sealed holdouts before interpreting removed mechanisms as redundant.

Construct a mechanism-count versus capability-coverage Pareto frontier. Report high-leverage mechanisms and observable nonlinear interactions using both-present/A-only/B-only/neither comparisons where supported.

## Adversarial review
After subset and holdout results, perform a hostile methodological review: duplicate capability families; interface-coupled necessity; state ownership mistakes; invalid construction masquerading as cognitive failure; omitted earned capabilities; holdouts accidentally adding capabilities; state migration into environment/test harness; indirect activation of disabled mechanisms; fixed-capacity violations; and cleaner ablation alternatives. Correct audit methodology only when justified. Never modify production v9.3.
